# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three equally weighted personas: the 30s professional buying their first real luxury watch (aspirational, values uniqueness over heritage names); the 40s collector adding something no one else has (craft-driven, technically discerning); and the high-budget gift buyer making an emotional milestone purchase. All three share one trait: they want something that was made for them, not pulled from a display case.

## Product Purpose

MERIDIAN is a made-to-order watch configurator. Buyers choose dial colour, case finish, and size in real time via a 3D model, then place an order that goes straight to assembly. No warehouse inventory. No compromise. The site exists to make that process feel like the purchase of a lifetime object, not an e-commerce transaction.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic. Dark. Considered.

The brand does not announce itself. It reveals itself — through motion, precision copy, and the weight of what it withholds. Every element on screen should feel like it was placed deliberately, not filled in.

Voice: expert, unhurried, exact. The brand knows things and doesn't need to prove it. No exclamation marks. No urgency tactics. No feature lists.

## Anti-references

- Swiss heritage brands (Rolex, AP, Patek): museum-like, static, history-heavy, backward-looking. MERIDIAN is the inverse: made-to-order and forward.
- Generic Shopify luxury stores: white background, product grid, gold serif headline, meaningless taglines. Looks like 1,000 other brands.
- SaaS configurator energy: feature cards, gradient CTAs, "Configure yours today!" copy. The interaction should feel like craft, not software.

## Design Principles

1. **Motion is material.** Animation is not decoration — it is the surface texture of the brand. Blur reveals, scroll-driven transitions, and particle ambience are part of the product.
2. **Withhold to create desire.** Negative space, dark backgrounds, and sparse copy are not absences — they are the luxury. Never fill space because it is empty.
3. **Every word was chosen.** Copy reads as if someone spent an hour on each sentence. No restated headings. No intros that summarize the section. The headline IS the thought.
4. **Precision over ornament.** Details earn their place through function or atmosphere. If a visual element serves neither, remove it.
5. **The object is the hero.** The 3D watch model is the site's centre of gravity. Everything else — copy, sections, motion — exists to frame it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA where practical. Dark palette means some contrast exceptions are unavoidable for purely decorative elements; interactive elements and body text must meet AAA. Reduced-motion variants required for all GSAP animations (prefers-reduced-motion already partially implemented). Keyboard navigation must reach all interactive states including configurator swatches, size selectors, and cart.
